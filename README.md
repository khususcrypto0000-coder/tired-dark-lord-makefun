A 100-word monologue for the make.fun bounty
"The Tired Dark Lord: A 100-Word Villain Monologue".

The speaker is no generic dark lord: he is the keeper of the dream
toll, stamping and filing one dream from every dreamer, night after
night, while the queue bends past the salt marshes and never ends.
His exhaustion is never announced — it leaks out through the rhythm,
the pauses, the sentences he abandons halfway, and the small
deflections ("sorry", "never mind") — while the menace stays: pay up,
hero, your sweetest dream, please.

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
```

It writes the monologue to `/output/entry.txt` — exactly 100 words,
under the 600-character gate, standard library only, deterministic.
